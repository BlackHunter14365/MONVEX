# MONVEX 2.0 Swiss Brutalist Design System & UI Architecture

## 1. Aesthetic Foundations
MONVEX 2.0 combines **Swiss Modernism** with **Fintech Precision**:
- **Typography**: Crisp typographic scale with high-contrast hierarchy and uppercase tracking labels (`swiss-eyebrow`).
- **Color Palette**:
  - Deep Navy Slate: `#172033` (Primary)
  - Warm Quartz Alabaster: `#F6F5F1` (Background)
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF` (Surface)
  - Neutral Line: `#E4E2DC` (Borders)
  - Emerald Green: `#059669` (Inflow / Positive Delta / Solvency)
  - Rose Crimson: `#E11D48` (Outflow / Debt / High Velocity)
  - Cobalt Blue: `#2563EB` (Intelligence / Telemetry)
- **Liquid Cards & Specular Top**: Subtle 1px specular light refraction with soft ambient shadows (`liquid-card specular-top`).

---

## 2. Interactive Navigation Architecture
The Sidebar categorizes financial management into clear cognitive domains:
1. **Overview**: Dashboard, Transactions, Budgets, Savings Goals, Analytics.
2. **Intelligence**: AI Copilot, What-If Simulator, Cashflow Forecast, Receipt Scanner.
3. **Wealth & Planning**: Net Worth & Balance Sheet, Debt & EMI Planner, Subscriptions, Monthly Statements.
4. **System & Security**: Smart Alerts, Cyber Defense & Security, Preferences.
